05 · Choosing K

How many topics?

LDA needs the number of topics K up front. One common guide is topic coherence Cv, which checks how often a topic's top words co-occur in a sliding window over the corpus (higher is more interpretable). Here K = 10 scored slightly higher than K = 15, but 15 separates subjects that 10 merges (grain vs. sugar, mergers vs. divestitures), so we kept 15. Coherence is a heuristic: always read the topics.

Per-topic coherence is on each card. Topics marked mixed (Cv < 0.45) blend unrelated stories; that's normal, and a sign that a different K, more passes or better preprocessing might help.

06 · Concepts

How it works

Generative story

For each topic k draw word distribution φₖ ~ Dir(η). For each document draw topic proportions θ ~ Dir(α); for each word position draw a topic z ~ Cat(θ) and then the word w ~ Cat(φ_z). Training inverts this story: given only the words, infer φ and θ.

Training here

gensim's online variational Bayes on 7,787 Reuters-21578 articles (those with at least 20 content words), 4,000-word vocabulary (stopwords and words in >30% or <10 documents removed), 10 passes, α and η learned from the data.

Inference in your browser

With φ fixed, collapsed Gibbs sampling resamples each token's topic with probability ∝ (nd,k + αₖ) · φk,w, where nd,k counts the document's other tokens assigned to k. θ is averaged over the second half of the sweeps.

Bag of words

Order is ignored: “oil prices fell as OPEC cut output” and a shuffled version get the same topics. LDA finds what documents are about, not what they say.

Successors

Correlated and dynamic topic models relax LDA's assumptions; neural approaches such as BERTopic cluster contextual sentence embeddings and then describe each cluster with class-based TF-IDF.
