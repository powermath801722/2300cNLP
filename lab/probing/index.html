02 · Concepts

How it works

Probing

Freeze the model, take its vector h for each word, and train a small supervised classifier f(h) → tag. If even a linear f reaches high accuracy on held-out data, the information is linearly decodable from h. Probes of this kind have been used for part of speech, syntactic depth, coreference and more (Belinkov 2022 surveys them).

Baselines matter

Most English words have one dominant tag. On a full treebank, looking up each word’s most frequent training tag already scores around 90% (see Jurafsky & Martin’s tagging chapter). Here, with only 400 training sentences and many unseen test words, the lookup is lower, and the results table shows it. A probe has to beat that lookup, not chance. The page compares the probe with the majority tag, that lookup, and a probe on random per-word vectors, which carry word identity and nothing else.

Control tasks and selectivity

Hewitt & Liang (2019) assign each word type a random fixed label drawn from the real tag distribution. A probe that scores well on this control task is memorizing word types, not reading linguistic structure. Their measure is selectivity: real-task accuracy minus control accuracy. Expressive probes (MLPs) raise both accuracies, so their selectivity is usually lower.

What probing can’t show

Decodable isn’t the same as used: the model may never rely on what the probe finds (Ravichander, Belinkov & Hovy 2021; Elazar et al. 2021, amnesic probing). Results depend on probe capacity and data size, so information-theoretic framings report the cost of decoding (Voita & Titov 2020, MDL probes; Pimentel et al. 2020). Causal methods intervene on the representation instead.

Caveats on this page

Only the last layer of a 6-layer model is probed. That model was fine-tuned for sentence similarity, which isn’t a pure language-model objective, and syntactic information is usually strongest in middle layers (Tenney, Das & Pavlick 2019). Training is 30 epochs of Adam on standardized features, with the L2 strength you choose; hyperparameters were not tuned on the test set. Numbers vary slightly with the random seed.
